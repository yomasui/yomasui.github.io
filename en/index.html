Tax Law & International Taxation

Yoshihiro Masui

My research and teaching focus on tax law.

Tax systems are closely tied to how states and societies are organized. How are tax rules made? How do they shape the behavior of businesses and individuals, and how are they put into practice?

My work has examined the design of tax systems, international taxation and business activity, and taxpayer behavior and tax enforcement. One question runs through this work: Why do people pay taxes?

Three Pillars of My Research

Tax Law and Tax Policy

I examine how public goods are financed from a legal perspective, with a focus on taxation.

International Taxation and Business

I study the challenges globalization poses for tax systems, including those involving multinational enterprises, tax treaties, and tax competition.

Taxpayer Behavior and Tax Enforcement

I explore why people pay taxes and how legal institutions and enforcement practices support tax compliance.

These three areas are closely connected. Designing tax systems requires an understanding of how businesses and individuals behave and how the law is enforced in practice.
